// queue flow

!open seq or !open ran → viewers type !join → mod types !draw → drawn players type !ready within 30s → confirmed players appear in the lobby.

Repeat !draw to fill more slots. Use !stop when done taking new players, or !reset to wipe everything and start fresh.

!stop closes the queue so no new players can join — but everyone already in the queue stays. The lobby stays intact too. You can reopen with !open and continue drawing.

!reset wipes everything: queue, lobby, and draw state. Everyone has to !join again from scratch.

!open seq — sequential. Players are drawn in the order they joined. First in, first out.

!open ran — random. Players are drawn randomly from the queue regardless of join order.

Yes — !draw 3 draws up to 3 players. They are prompted one at a time: each has 30 seconds to !ready before the next is called. You can draw up to 5 at once.

// ready-up

They have 30 seconds to type !ready. If the timer runs out, their slot is lost and the next player in the drawn batch is prompted.

They are automatically put back at the end of the queue — no need to !join again.

!skip passes your slot and puts you back at the end of the queue automatically. You can be drawn again in a future !draw.

Mods can also skip on a player's behalf.

The lobby shows players who have confirmed their slot with !ready. It persists across multiple !draw rounds so you always know who is already in.

The lobby clears on !nextround (players moved back to queue) or !reset (everything wiped).

!nextround — moves everyone in the lobby back to the end of the queue and clears the lobby. Players who were waiting in queue the whole time stay at the front. Fair for everyone.

Use !reset instead if you want a completely fresh start with no one in queue.

// activity check

Players who haven't chatted in the last 10 minutes are skipped when drawn. This prevents AFK players from holding up the lobby. They stay in the queue — they just need to type something to become active again.
